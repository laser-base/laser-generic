**Reading the custom hub-and-spoke scenario plot.**
A free-form spatial layout in meters, axes roughly -150,000 to +150,000 on both x and y — built from a hand-assembled GeoDataFrame rather than `grid()`. Each node is drawn as a filled circle whose marker reflects its geographic buffer radius, colored by population on a `viridis` scale (colorbar 0 to 1.0 x 10^6).

A single bright yellow circle sits at the origin — the Central City of 1,000,000 with a 10 km buffer. Five medium dark-purple circles ring it at radius 150,000 m, evenly spaced at 72-degree increments (Town 1 east on the +x axis, then four more rotated around) — these are the surrounding towns of 100,000 each, also with 10 km buffers. Between the hub and each town sit two smaller dark-purple circles at radii 50,000 and 100,000 — the "Interim 1a/1b" intermediate centers of 25,000 with 5 km buffers. The radial spokes are visually obvious as five chains of four nodes each. **The figure demonstrates that LASER scenarios are not restricted to `grid()` output: any GeoDataFrame with `nodeid`, `population`, and `geometry` columns will do, enabling bespoke hub-and-spoke topologies that the regular lattice constructor cannot express.**
