### Reading the infectious-snapshots map grid

A 3 x 4 grid of small spatial maps, one per panel, titled `Infectious at tick 0`, `30`, `60`, …, `330` (12 snapshots, 30-tick stride). Each panel reuses the hub-and-spoke geometry from the previous plots — Central City at the origin, five towns on a radius-150 km pentagon, two interim nodes per spoke — with nodes colored by current `I` count on a `Reds` colormap and a per-panel colorbar (each panel auto-scales).

At tick 0 a single dark-red dot lights up on the east spoke — Town 1, the seeded node. By tick 30-60 the same node is still the only visible focus. The colorbar maximum climbs steadily: ~100 at tick 0, ~2000 at tick 90, peaking near 140,000-175,000 around ticks 180-210 (still concentrated in Town 1), then declining back toward ~500 by tick 330. Faint pink halos appear on neighboring interim and town nodes from tick ~150 onward, signaling slow gravity-driven spillover, but Town 1 remains the dominant burn for the entire run. **The figure demonstrates the spatial-temporal story of the SEIR run: the outbreak ignites in a single seeded town and stays largely confined there because the gravity coupling is weak enough that the epidemic finishes locally before meaningful diffusion reaches the central hub.**
